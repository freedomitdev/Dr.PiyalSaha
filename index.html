Care that starts with listening.

Dr. Piyal Saha is a physician who consults patients at Ekushey Digital Diagnostic Center and Model Hospital in Hajiganj, Chandpur.

He completed his MBBS in Cumilla and has continued his medical training with several further qualifications.

His approach is built on unhurried conversations, plain explanations and plans that patients genuinely understand and can follow.
